[4] O. Sufyan and O. Neufeld, "Double circular dichroism high harmonic spectroscopy: An ultrafast probe for topological photocurrents," arXiv:2604.21121 [cond-mat.other] (2026). 预印本。Haldane 纳米片上提出双圆二色性（DCD）方法，以双圆偏振解耦体态与边缘态的拓扑光电流贡献。

[5] S. Li, W. Zhou, K. A. Imroz, Y. Tang, T. A. Townsend, V. Leshchenko, L. Boie, P. Agostini, A. S. Landsman, R. K. Kawakami, L. Yue, and L. F. [6] D. Bauer and K. K. Hansen, "High-harmonic generation in solids with and without topological edge states," Phys. Rev. Lett. 120, 177401 (2018); DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.120.177401. SSH 拓扑相带隙下方谐波产额比平庸相最高增强达 14 个数量级，提出边界态 HHG 探针的雏形。

[7] H. Jürß and D. Bauer, "High-harmonic generation in Su-Schrieffer-Heeger chains," Phys. Rev. B 99, 195428 (2019); DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevB.99.195428. 在同一 SSH 模型上逐一比较体相与边界态对谐波谱的贡献，确认拓扑边界态是 HHG 的增强源。

[8] C.-T. Liu, J.-S. You, and H.-C. Hsu, "Even-harmonic generation from topological edge states in generalized Su-Schrieffer-Heeger models," arXiv:2608.22136 [cond-mat.mes-hall] (2026). 预印本。扩展 SSH 与 Rice-Mele 模型中，局域光照移至链边界时拓扑相六阶以上偶次谐波增强、奇次谐波近乎消失。

[9] C. S. Lange and L. B. Madsen, "Edge states and quantum optical high-harmonic generation from topological insulators," arXiv:2602.16454 [quant-ph] (2026). 预印本。压缩度（quadrature squeezing）在短链中可区分拓扑相，谐波谱在所有链长下均可区分拓扑相，两种探针互补。

[10] M. L. Bera, J. O. de Almeida, M. Dziurawiec, M. Płodzień, M. M. Maśka, M. Lewenstein, T. Graß, and U. [11] X. Zhang, J. van den Brink, and J. Li, "Impact of crystal symmetries and Weyl nodes on high-harmonic generation in Weyl semimetal TaAs," arXiv:2405.16125 [cond-mat.mtrl-sci] (2024). 预印本。以 Jones 矩阵形式体系分析 TaAs 的 HHG 选择定则，反常偶次谐波由 C₂(z) 与破缺镜面 M_z 的晶体对称性主导，而非 Weyl 锥拓扑本身。

[12] P. Pilch, C. Zhu, S. Kovalev, R. M. A. Dantas, A. Bedoya-Pinto, S. S. P. Parkin, and Z. Wang, "Terahertz third-harmonic generation of lightwave driven Weyl fermions far from equilibrium," Nano Lett. 25, 16637–16642 (2025); DOI: 10.1021/acs.nanolett.5c04143; arXiv:2508.16166 [cond-mat.str-el]. [13] R. K. Thakur, P. Venkat, A. Bharti, and G. Dixit, "Helicity-engineered nonlinear optical responses in photo-excited topological semimetals," arXiv:2609.06119 [physics.optics] (2026). 预印本。双圆极化泵浦-探测架构下 Weyl 半金属 HHG 的螺旋性敏感度，可作为电子-空穴退相干时钟。

[14] B. Lorenzo, C. Batista, M. Jangjan, D. Kim, J. Menotti, F. Liu, W. Gao, S. Ghimire, C. Granados, and A. [15] Y. S. You, D. A. Reis, and S. Ghimire, "Anisotropic high-harmonic generation in bulk crystals," Nat. Phys. 13, 345–349 (2017); DOI: 10.1038/nphys3955. MgO 单晶中 HHG 的晶体取向依赖给出绕 (001) 面法向旋转的四重角分布，构成纯对称性各向异性的基准曲线。

[16] M. F. Ciappina, "Solid-state high-order harmonic generation: Emerging frontiers in ultrafast and quantum light science," Adv. Phys. 74, 1–9 (2025); DOI: 10.1080/00018732.2025.2598130; arXiv:2510.15207 [physics.optics]. 综述，整合 Berry 相位与拓扑效应、激子与晶格运动、量子光驱动 HHG 三个前沿方向。

[17] L. Medic, J. Mravlje, A. Ramšak, and T. Rejec, "High-harmonic generation in semi-Dirac and Weyl semimetals with broken time-reversal symmetry: Exploration of the merging of Weyl nodes," Phys. [19] S. Duan, L. Shi, P. Pilch, A. Reinold, S. Kovalev, R. M. A. Dantas, Y. Yang, F. Xiu, M. S. Vitiello, and Z.
