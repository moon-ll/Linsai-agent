# Symmetry-Enabled Ferroelectric Valley Transduction in Rhombohedral Bilayer WSe$_2$

**arXiv**: [2611.15494](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15494)
**作者**: Katsunori Wakabayashi, Souren Adhikary, Tomoaki Kameda
**方向**: 能谷电子学
**deepxiv 评分**: 0.5

## TL;DR

## 摘要

Ferroelectric polarization couples to the layer degree of freedom in a bilayer, but a valley-selective energy response requires the valleys to carry different interlayer character. Rhombohedral bilayer WSe$_2$ meets that condition by symmetry: three-fold rotation forbids interlayer tunneling at the $K$ valley, while strong hybridization remains at $Γ$. The resulting contrast converts the band-effective ferroelectric layer asymmetry into a shift of the $K$ edge, and so displaces the $K$--$Γ$ landscape: a finite-field fit that does not impose the rule leaves the $K$ coupling consistent with zero, and the shift reaches 41.7 meV, the most that asymmetry can give a single valley. Reversing the polarization mirrors the valley landscape in displacement field, so the two registries have equal and opposite differential transconductance $g_d$ at zero field. In the ideal symmetric limit a second parity holds about each registry's own compensation field and places a zero of $g_d$ there. The valley-dependent screening calculated here displaces that zero by only 0.2 % at the read point, so device asymmetry limits the accuracy. The result links a known stacking selection rule to ferroelectric valley transduction, with a quantitative valley shift and a directly testable transport signature.
