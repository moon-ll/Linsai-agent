# Charge Filling-Dependent Valley Polarization and its Dynamics Driven by Correlated Interlayer Exciton States in WSe2/WS2 Heterobilayer

## 摘要

Moiré heterostructures of transition metal dichalcogenides provide an emerging platform for studying the strongly correlated electronic and excitonic states. The valley degree of freedom and its dynamics in the strongly correlated electronic and excitonic states in the moiré system are crucial for fundamental understanding and applications of valleytronics. Although the strongly correlated states in moiré systems have been extensively investigated, their impact on valley degrees of freedom and its dynamics remains unexplored. Here, we study the charge filling-dependent valley relaxation dynamics of interlayer excitons (IXs) in WSe2/WS2 moiré heterobilayer system. The evolution of moiré IXs dynamics across the Mott transition is elucidated by combining time-resolved optical spectroscopy with Monte Carlo simulation. Our results show that the radiative decay and recombination rate of IXs are enhanced at the integer filling factors n (=-1). Moreover, the valley relaxation rate of IXs at n=$\pm$1 is significantly decreased. The resulting reduction in wavefunction overlap and phonon scattering process suppresses the nonradiative recombination and intervalley scattering of IXs. These anomalous behaviors are attributed to the modification of IXs recombination pathways and enhance excitonic coherence due to the localization of introduced charge carriers at integer filling of moiré site. Our results explore exciton and valley dynamics as sensitive probes of strongly correlated states in moiré systems and highlight possible ways to control excitonic coherence and valleytronic properties in two-dimensional quantum materials.
