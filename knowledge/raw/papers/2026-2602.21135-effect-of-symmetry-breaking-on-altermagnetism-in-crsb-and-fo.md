# Effect of symmetry breaking on altermagnetism in CrSb and Formation of Fragmented Nodal Curves

DFT analysis of pristine, vacancy-engineered, and doped CrSb shows symmetry breaking yields fragmented nodal curves (FNCs) in momentum space, with strain engineering offering a route to finite AHC in CrSb.
