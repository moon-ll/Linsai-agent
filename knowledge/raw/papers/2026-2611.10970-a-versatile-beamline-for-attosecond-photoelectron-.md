# A versatile beamline for attosecond photoelectron and photon spectroscopy

## 摘要

We report on the development of a versatile attosecond beamline for ultrafast spectroscopy of atoms and molecules, as well as for the metrology of infrared laser pulses. Using a few-cycle pulse (approximately 5 fs) for driving the high-order harmonic generation process, we show that the modulation of the harmonic flux in the cut-off spectral region, induced by a weak perturbing field, enables complete electric-field characterization of the perturbing pulse. Phase-sensitive detection with a lock-in amplifier further reveals polarization-dependent effects, observed as modulations at twice the fundamental optical frequency, emphasizing the sub-laser-cycle electronic control inherent to the measurement technique. In addition, the beamline supports the implementation of two-color (extreme ultraviolet and infrared) phase-sensitive experiments using approximately 30 fs pulses and a velocity map imaging spectrometer (VMI). This configuration will enable the study of photoemission time delays in atomic and molecular systems. Together, these results establish the beamline as a robust and adaptable platform for probing attosecond dynamics across diverse experimental approaches.
