# Continuous relativistic high-harmonic generation from a kHz liquid-sheet plasma mirror

**arXiv**: [2410.15404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.15404)
**作者**: Antoine Cavagna, Milo Eder, Enam Chowdhury
**方向**: 阿秒科学
**deepxiv 评分**: 0.625

## TL;DR

[Experimental physics letter] The paper reports the first experimental observation of continuous high-harmonic generation (HHG) at 1 kHz from a relativistic-intensity liquid-sheet plasma mirror, achieved through precise control of the surface plasma density gradient via prepulse timing. By using waveform-controlled near-single-cycle laser pulses and spatially homogeneous plasma expansion, the authors measure the gradient scale length and demonstrate highly stable, reproducible extreme ultraviolet quasi-continua—enabling the generation of kHz-trains of isolated attosecond pulses—confirming liquid sheets as viable, high-repetition-rate platforms for future attosecond light sources.

## 摘要

We report on continuous high-harmonic generation at 1 kHz repetition rate from a liquid-sheet plasma mirror driven by relativistic-intensity near-single-cycle light transients. Through precise control of both the surface plasma density gradient and the driving light waveform, we can produce highly stable and reproducible extreme ultraviolet spectral quasi-continua, corresponding to the generation of stable kHz-trains of isolated attosecond pulses in the time domain. This confirms the exciting potential of liquid sheet targets as one of the building blocks of future high-power attosecond lasers.
