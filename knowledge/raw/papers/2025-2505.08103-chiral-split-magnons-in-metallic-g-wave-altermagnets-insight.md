# Chiral split magnons in metallic g-wave altermagnets: Insights from many-body perturbation theory

DFT+many-body perturbation theory analysis of MAs/MZ (M=V,Cr; Z=As,Sb,Bi) altermagnets reveals how pnictogen substitution affects spin splitting and chiral magnon dispersions, with CrSb showing large chiral magnon splitting.
