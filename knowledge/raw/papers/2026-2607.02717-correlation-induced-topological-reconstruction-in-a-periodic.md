# Correlation-Induced Topological Reconstruction in a Periodically Driven Kagome Mott Insulator

Brillouin-Wigner perturbation theory on Kagome Hubbard model with circular drive derives an effective Floquet Hamiltonian that produces repeated topological transitions and a tunable flat band. Slave-rotor mean-field shows non-monotonic Mott transition with metal-insulator re-entries; spinon bands acquire topology distinct from non-interacting Floquet bands. Direct match for CDW Floquet engineering and Mott insulator periodic drive control.
