# Ultrafast Interlayer Sliding Dynamics in Ferroelectric Bilayers

Pump-probe experiments combined with first-principles calculations reveal sub-picosecond interlayer sliding in bilayer h-BN-like ferroelectrics, demonstrating that electric-field-driven stacking reorganization can occur on ultrafast timescales and potentially enabling THz-speed non-volatile memory.
