# Excitonic effects on high-harmonic generation in Mott insulators

Extended-Hubbard-model TDED/iTEBD calculation showing excitons in1D Mott insulators strongly enhance HHG at the exciton energy, with subcycle intensity splitting from Stark oscillation — direct evidence of bound-state V effects in HHG spectra.
