# On-demand valley control in a WSe2/CrPS4 heterostructure via optically programmed magnetic order

**arXiv**: [2611.18906](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18906)
**作者**: Muhammad Hassan Shaikh, Daria Blach, Harishankar Jayakumar
**方向**: 能谷电子学

## 摘要

The ability to optically control the valley degree of freedom in two-dimensional semiconductors is critical for scalable valleytronics. While magnetic proximity effects can lift valley degeneracy, static interfaces lack the dynamic control needed for devices. Here, we demonstrate on-demand optical switching of valley symmetry breaking in a WSe2/CrPS4 heterostructure by exploiting light-tunable magnetic order. Using polarization- and time-resolved photoluminescence, we show that excitation below the ligand-to-metal charge transfer (LMCT) gap preserves an out-of-plane Néel vector in CrPS4, generating a net out-of-plane spin at the interface and enabling spin-polarized charge transfer. This yields a robust polarization gap in the WSe2 A-exciton decay dynamics without an external magnetic field. Excitation above the LMCT gap extinguishes the polarization gap, indicating a rotation of the Néel vector to in-plane. This reversible switching provides direct evidence of spin-polarized charge transfer. Furthermore, the same optical excitation enables optical control of the decay dynamics of the low-energy excitons (XLE) by ~42%, which is potentially attributed to the modification of interfacial couplings. Our work establishes a first unique multifunctional platform where magnetic symmetry, valley polarization, and exciton relaxation are integrated and dynamically controlled by light.
