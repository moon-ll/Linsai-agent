# Cost-Effective Real-Time TDDFT for Twisted Heterostructures via Adaptive Subspace Embedding

Combines rt-TDDFT with adaptive subspace tight-binding embedding to simulate excited-state dynamics in twisted heterostructures, achieving >10× core-hour reduction over full rt-TDDFT on twisted bilayer graphene supercells.
