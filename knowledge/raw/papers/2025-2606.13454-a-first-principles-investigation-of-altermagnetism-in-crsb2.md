# A first-principles investigation of altermagnetism in CrSb2 under applied pressure

GGA+U DFT shows CrSb2 exhibits d-wave altermagnetic spin splitting of ~0.5 eV near EF, with collinear AFM ground state confirmed by magnetic susceptibility under hydrostatic pressure.
