# Engineering photomagnetism in collinear van der Waals antiferromagnets

Demonstrates that 10% Ni substitution in MnPS3 enhances coherent photomagnetism by over an order of magnitude while preserving the Néel ground state, with Ni2+ 3A1g multiplet as the most efficient magnon driver due to its localized nature and orbital angular momentum.
