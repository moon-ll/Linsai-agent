# High harmonic spectroscopy reveals anisotropy of the charge-density-wave phase transition in TiSe2

Experimental polarization-resolved HHG spectroscopy of 1T-TiSe2 from 300K to 14K reveals an anisotropic HHG component emerging below T_CDW~200K, demonstrating HHG as a macroscopic probe of competing excitonic/electron-phonon CDW mechanisms.
