# Attosecond Light Skyrmion Pulses via High Harmonic Generation

**作者**: David Marco, Luis Plaja, Carlos Hernández-García
**方向**: 阿秒科学

## TL;DR

[short communication (Letter)] The paper demonstrates that attosecond skyrmion pulses can be generated via high-harmonic generation (HHG) by using a linearly polarized vector beam with fractional orbital angular momentum (OAM) composed of two counter-rotating circularly polarized components. Simulations show that the harmonic spectrum preserves nearly identical skyrmion polarization textures across multiple harmonics, enabling coherent superposition into a train of attosecond pulses with ~500-attosecond duration and Skyrme number ≈1, experimentally realizable with 1.2 μm drivers and spectral filtering.

## 摘要

Paraxial light skyrmions are topological configurations that map a spatial domain of the field onto the full Poincar\\'e sphere of polarization states. While optical skyrmions have been explored in continuous-wave regimes, their realization in the ultrafast domain remains open. Here we demonstrate that attosecond skyrmion pulses can be generated via high-harmonic generation. Advanced simulations combining single-atom strong-field theory and macroscopic propagation reveal that an infrared linearly polarized vector beam with fractional orbital angular momentum produces extreme-ultraviolet harmonic fields with nearly identical skyrmion polarization distributions across a broad spectral range. Using 1.2 $\\mu$m driving fields and experimentally feasible spectral filtering, we show that the coherent superposition of consecutive harmonics centered at 70 eV yields a train of skyrmion pulses with $\\sim500$ attoseconds duration. Our results open opportunities to use structured attosecond light with topological polarization textures in fields as ultrafast control, imaging and spectroscopy.
