# Ultrafast Successive Control and Electrical Detection of Long-Lived Valley Polarization in Room-Temperature Germanium

## 摘要

Germanium (Ge) hosts four degenerate valleys in the conduction band minima, and their highly anisotropic effective masses offer a valley degree of freedom in transport phenomena via anisotropic conductivity. Using time- and polarization-resolved terahertz spectroscopy combined with deformation potential theory, we reveal a valley relaxation time reaching 10 ps near the band edge of Ge(001) at room temperature, long enough to detect the valley polarization in electrical transport measurements. We also demonstrate sub-picosecond complete reversal of valley polarization by off-resonant light pulses at extremely low energy consumption. These results pave the way for room-temperature valleytronics based on bulk indirect-gap semiconductors compatible with existing electronics.
