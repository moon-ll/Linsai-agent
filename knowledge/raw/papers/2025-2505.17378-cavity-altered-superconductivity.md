# Cavity-altered superconductivity

Hexagonal boron nitride hyperbolic modes are interfaced with the molecular superconductor κ-(BEDT-TTF)2Cu[N(CN)2]Br (Tc=11.5 K); resonant hBN-C=C vibrational coupling alters the superfluid density at the interface.
