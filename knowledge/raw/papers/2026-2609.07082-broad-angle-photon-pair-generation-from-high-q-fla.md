# Broad-angle photon-pair generation from high-$Q$ flatband resonant 3R-MoS$_{2}$ metasurfaces

**arXiv**: [2609.07082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07082)
**作者**: Tingting Liu, Huifu Qiu, Xintong Shi
**方向**: 固体HHG
**deepxiv 评分**: 0.5

## TL;DR

## 摘要

Spontaneous parametric down-conversion (SPDC) in ultrathin optical resonant metasurfaces offers a promising platform for integrated quantum light sources. However, conventional resonances suffer from steep momentum dispersion, limiting photon-pair generation to narrow excitation and emission angles. Here, we demonstrate a high-$Q$ flatband resonant 3R-MoS$_{2}$ metasurface for high-efficiency, broad-angle SPDC photon-pair generation. By combining Brillouin zone folding with in-plane symmetry breaking, we engineer a high-$Q$ flatband resonance in the near-infrared regime, maintaining a robust $Q$-factor across a wide range of incident angles. Exploiting the strong local electric field enhancement of resonance and the large second-order susceptibility of 3R-MoS$_{2}$, the metasurface boosts the degenerate SPDC pair-generation rate with orders of magnitude enhancement over an unpatterned film of identical thickness. Crucially, the near-zero band dispersion extends the effective emission angle to $\pm 5^{\circ}$ with negligible spectral drift $\sim 0.1$ nm and high directional tolerance. Under pulsed excitation with a finite spectral bandwidth, the metasurface maintains a substantial bandwidth-averaged photon-pair yield, while demonstrating strong spectral locking of the resonant emission channel against pump wavelength detuning despite the expected trade-off in generation efficiency. This work establishes a versatile strategy for momentum-independent quantum light sources, opening new avenues for scan-free quantum ghost imaging and quantum information processing.
