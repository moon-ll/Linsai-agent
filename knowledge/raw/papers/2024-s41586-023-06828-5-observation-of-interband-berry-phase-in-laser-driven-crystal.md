# Observation of interband Berry phase in laser-driven crystals

Introduces a conceptually new manifestation of the Berry phase — the interband Berry phase — accumulated during a discrete evolution between different bands in light-driven crystals. Using attosecond interferometry in α-quartz z-cut crystal driven by a 1.2-μm mid-IR field, engineers an internal k-space interferometer induced within less than one optical cycle that maps the Berry phase onto HHG emission, enabling direct probing of Berry curvature in trivial insulators on attosecond timescales (Nature 626, 66-71).
