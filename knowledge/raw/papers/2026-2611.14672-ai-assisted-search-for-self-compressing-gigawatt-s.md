# AI-assisted search for self-compressing gigawatt subcycle field waveforms

**arXiv**: [2611.14672](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14672)
**作者**: Aleksei M. Zheltikov, Youchan Park, Petr Zhokhov
**方向**: 阿秒科学
**deepxiv 评分**: 0.5

## TL;DR

## 摘要

We explore the physics behind pulse self-compression to high-power subcycle field waveforms and search for the optimal regimes of such compression over the parameter space as laid out by multimode nonlinear dynamics of ultrashort laser pulses in gas-filled hollow fibers. With the pertinent physical criteria for pulse contrast and no pulse breakup formalized as operational stopping rules, a radical speedup of such search is achieved by delegating its tedious preliminary stages to generative pretrained transformer models. The precise adjustment of self-compression dynamics vis-a-vis the desired compressed pulse output is, however, not yet delegable to AI agents, as the criteria for such search are still largely subjective, operating with fine aspects of subcycle field structure, not yet formalizable as clear search-stopping instructions. An ultimate precision in such subcycle field engineering is attainable via a management of field-phase-sensitive nonlinear processes, enabling pulse self-compression to high-power subcycle field waveforms and opening paths toward fiber-optic attosecond lightwave engineering.
