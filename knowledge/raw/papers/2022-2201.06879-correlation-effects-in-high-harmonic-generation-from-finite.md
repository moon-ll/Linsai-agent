# Correlation effects in high-harmonic generation from finite systems

Hubbard-model study of finite-size and electron-correlation interplay in HHG: weak U gives low-order finite-lattice enhancement and high-order correlational enhancement; near the Mott limit (U~0.8t_0) correlation enhancement dominates the full spectrum.
