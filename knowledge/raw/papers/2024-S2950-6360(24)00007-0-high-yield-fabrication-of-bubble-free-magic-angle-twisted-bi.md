# High-yield fabrication of bubble-free magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene devices with high twist-angle homogeneity

Optimized tear-and-stack protocol yields ~38% of devices with mm² regions at θ=1.1°±0.1° and twist-angle variation Δθ≈0.02°, enabling statistically robust device ensembles.
