# Proximity-induced Rashba spin-orbit interaction in BaMnO3|KTaO3 heterostructure for antiferromagnetic spintronics

First-principles design of BaMnO3|KTaO3 heterostructure demonstrating proximity-induced strong Rashba SOC from KTaO3 Ta-5d states to the robust antiferromagnetic BaMnO3 Mn-3d states, addressing the challenge of combining antiferromagnetism with Rashba interaction.
