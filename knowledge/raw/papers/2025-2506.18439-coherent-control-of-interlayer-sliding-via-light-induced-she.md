# Coherent Control of Interlayer Sliding via Light-Induced Shear Phonons in Ferroelectric Bilayers

Experiment-theory study demonstrating that tailored THz pulses can drive coherent interlayer shear phonons to controllably toggle the polarization state of sliding ferroelectric bilayers, achieving partial switching within the duration of a single pulse.
