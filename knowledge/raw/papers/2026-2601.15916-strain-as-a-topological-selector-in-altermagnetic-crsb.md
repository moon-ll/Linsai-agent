# Strain as a topological selector in altermagnetic CrSb

DFT calculations show isotropic tensile strain (4-5%) and effective Hubbard U_eff can selectively stabilize Weyl, Dirac, and triple-point fermions in altermagnetic Weyl semimetal CrSb, with a 3D low-energy Hamiltonian capturing the interaction-driven topology.
