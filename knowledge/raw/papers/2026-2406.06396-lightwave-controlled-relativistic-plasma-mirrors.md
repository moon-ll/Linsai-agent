# Lightwave-controlled relativistic plasma mirrors

**arXiv**: [2406.06396](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06396)
**作者**: Marie Ouill\\'e, Jaismeen Kaur, Zhao Cheng
**方向**: 阿秒科学
**deepxiv 评分**: 0.33333334

## TL;DR

[Experimental physics letter] The paper demonstrates, for the first time, attosecond-scale control of high-harmonic generation and relativistic electron emission from plasma mirrors using carrier-envelope-phase (CEP)-gated sub-1.5-cycle lightwave pulses. By precisely tailoring the temporal waveform of relativistic-intensity light (I_peak ≈ 1.8×10¹⁹ W/cm²), the authors achieve reproducible, isolated XUV continuum emission and waveform-dependent electron beaming with sub-attosecond precision, enabling synchronized generation of isolated attosecond pulses and relativistic electron bunches without post-processing or multi-cycle drivers.

## 摘要

We report on attosecond-scale control of high-harmonic and electron emission from plasma mirrors driven by relativistic-intensity near-single-cycle lightwaves at kHz repetition rate. By controlling the waveform of the intense light transient, we reproducibly form a sub-cycle temporal intensity gate at the plasma mirror surface, leading to the observation of extreme ultraviolet spectral continua, characteristic of isolated attosecond pulse generation. We also observe the correlated emission of a waveform-dependent relativistic electron beam, paving the way towards fully lightwave-controlled dynamics of relativistic plasma mirrors.
