# Effective-Rank Regularization for Ultrafast Multi-Dimensional Inversion

Introduces an effective-rank-based regularization scheme for ill-posed attosecond inversion, demonstrating improved convergence on synthetic and experimental HHG datasets and characterizing the parameter-dimension scaling with noise level.
