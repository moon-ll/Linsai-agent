# Correlation-induced generation of superharmonics in the high-order harmonic spectrum of perovskite barium titanate

TDDFT with DMFT-derived XC kernel on BaTiO3 shows electron-electron correlations shift HHG spectral weight to higher frequencies and produce correlation-strength-dependent superharmonic peaks from effective-correlation-energy Hubbard subband transitions.
