# Chasing the spin gap through the phase diagram of a frustrated Mott insulator

High-resolution strain tuning of κ-(BEDT-TTF)2Cu2(CN)3 maps a spin-singlet insulating phase with 30-50 K spin gap (the enigmatic 6 K anomaly) and identifies it as a valence-bond-solid transition that persists until unconventional superconductivity sets in.
