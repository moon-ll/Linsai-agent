# Flat band driven competing charge and spin instabilities in the altermagnet CrSb

X-ray diffuse scattering and DFT reveal short-range charge-order fluctuations at the M point above TN in CrSb, with a giant spin-phonon coupling of ~6 meV—the largest reported—signaling flat-band-driven competing order parameters.
