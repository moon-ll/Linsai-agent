# High-harmonic spectroscopy of strongly bound excitons in solids

Ab-initio 1D-solid simulations show a finite bound-exciton population imprints a peak at ω_ex plus ±2ω sidebands in HHG spectra following a power-law N_ex^0.92, enabling optical readout of exciton population.
