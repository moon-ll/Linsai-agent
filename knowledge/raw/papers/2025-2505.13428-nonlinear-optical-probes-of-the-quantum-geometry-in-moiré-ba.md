# Nonlinear Optical Probes of the Quantum Geometry in Moiré Bands

Reviews and extends the theory of nonlinear optical responses in moiré flat bands, identifying specific pump-probe geometries that isolate the quantum metric and Berry connection from second-harmonic and sum-frequency signals for experimental readout.
