# Landau theory and exchange instabilities in Mn5Si3: A case against altermagnetism

Thin-film Mn5Si3 is one of the most studied altermagnetic candidates. Its postulated altermagnetic structure is examined via Landau theory, revealing exchange instabilities that challenge the altermagnetic interpretation.
