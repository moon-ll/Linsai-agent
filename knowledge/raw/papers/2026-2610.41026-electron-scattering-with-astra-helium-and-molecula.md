## 摘要

We present an implementation of electron-impact scattering in \ASTRA{}~(AttoSecond TRAnsitions)~[J. M. Randazzo {\it et al.}, Phys. Rev. Research {\bf 5}, 043115 (2023)], a general close-coupling method for the molecular electronic continuum based on transition density matrices and already employed for molecular photoionization. The scattering states are obtained with a pole-free Galerkin method with variational surface matching, which yields the scattering matrix and, from it, the elastic and electronically inelastic integral cross sections and the molecular-frame differential cross sections. For helium, \ASTRA{} reproduces elastic and inelastic cross sections over a broad energy range, resolves the $\mathrm{He}^{-}(1s2s^2\,{}^2S^e)$ Feshbach resonance in both energy and angle, and reproduces the interference features in the angular distributions for $1s^2\to1s 2\ell$ excitations observed experimentally. For molecular hydrogen, the elastic and inelastic cross sections closely reproduce the benchmark results obtained with \UKRmolplus{} for the same close-coupling expansion. We also report orientation-resolved, three-dimensional molecular-frame angular distributions for state-resolved electron-impact excitation, which are shown to depend strongly on the molecular orientation. These results validate the method in view of its application to larger molecular targets.
