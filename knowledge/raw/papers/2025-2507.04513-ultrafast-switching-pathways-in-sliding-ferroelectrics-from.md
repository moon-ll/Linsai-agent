# Ultrafast Switching Pathways in Sliding Ferroelectrics: From First-Principles to Petahertz Logic

Review and new first-principles NEB calculations of switching barriers in sliding ferroelectric bilayers showing that collective interlayer shear modes can potentially drive polarization reversal on sub-100-femtosecond timescales if coherently excited by tailored light fields.
