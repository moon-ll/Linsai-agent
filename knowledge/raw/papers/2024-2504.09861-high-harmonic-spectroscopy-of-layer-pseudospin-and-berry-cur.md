# High-Harmonic Spectroscopy of Layer-Pseudospin and Berry Curvature in Homobilayers

Proposes using the polarization and ellipticity dependence of high harmonics generated in homobilayer TMDs to extract the layer-resolved Berry curvature and thereby image layer-pseudospin textures with sub-cycle temporal resolution.
