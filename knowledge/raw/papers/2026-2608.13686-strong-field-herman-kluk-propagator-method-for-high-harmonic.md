# Strong-field Herman-Kluk propagator method for high-harmonic generation in molecules

We extend our semiclassical strong-field Herman-Kluk (SFHK) propagator method to calculate high-order harmonic generation (HHG) in diatomic molecules driven by few-cycle intense laser fields.
