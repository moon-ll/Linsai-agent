# Strain- and Electric-Field-Tunable Valley Polarization in Mo0.75V0.25Te2(Mo3VTe8) for Valleytronic Application

## 摘要
Valley polarization in 2D TMDs is promising for low-power valleytronic and spin-valley information processing, but time-reversal symmetry in pristine nonmagnetic TMDs keeps the K+ and K- valleys degenerate. In this work, we investigated the structural stability, electronic properties, and tunable valley polarization of V-alloyed MoTe2 monolayer, Mo0.75V0.25Te2, using first-principles DFT calculations. Substitutional alloying of MoTe2 with V introduced magnetic exchange interaction, which, together with spin-orbit coupling (SOC), lifted the valley degeneracy. The alloyed structure was found to be energetically and dynamically stable. In pristine MoTe2, SOC produced spin splittings of 34.0 meV and 218.9 meV in the conduction and valence bands, but no valley polarization. In contrast, Mo0.75V0.25Te2 exhibited spontaneous valley polarization of 37.3 meV in the conduction band and 78.2 meV in the valence band, further enhanced by external electric fields and biaxial strain. A transverse electric field along the crystal c axis produced the maximum valley splitting of 132.8 meV in the valence band.

## 背景概述与问题引入
非磁性 TMD 的时间反演对称保护 K+/K- 谷简并，谷极化需要磁交换。问题：V 合金化 MoTe2 单层（Mo0.75V0.25Te2）能否稳定存在并产生可调自发谷极化？应变/电场如何增强？

## 关键发现及核心讨论
1. **自发谷极化**：Mo0.75V0.25Te2 展现自发谷极化——导带 37.3 meV、价带 78.2 meV（V 磁交换 + SOC 联合解简并）。
2. **电场/应变增强**：横向电场（沿 c 轴）使价带谷劈裂最大达 132.8 meV；双轴应变进一步调节。
3. **稳定性确认**：合金结构能量与动力学稳定（无虚频声子模）。
4. **基线对照**：纯 MoTe2 中 SOC 自旋劈裂（导带 34.0 meV/价带 218.9 meV）但不产生谷极化。

## 主要方法
- **DFT 第一性原理**：V 合金 MoTe2 的结构稳定性、电子结构、谷极化计算 + 电场/双轴应变扫描。

## 创新性
把 V 合金化确立为 MoTe2 谷极化的稳定且强可调（电场 132.8 meV）路线。

## 结论与展望
对林赛方向：电场可调谷劈裂（>130 meV）在强场实验中可被光场瞬态模拟/增强；该材料是磁场-free 谷极化 HHG 实验的候选体系。
