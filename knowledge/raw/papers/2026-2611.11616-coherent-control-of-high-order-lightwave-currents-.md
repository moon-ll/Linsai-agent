## 摘要

The sub-cycle acceleration of charge carriers in an electronic circuit by tailored terahertz (THz) electric-field waveforms, rather than conventional pulsed bias voltages, opens a transformative frontier for lightwave electronics operating far beyond the scaling limits of gigahertz electronic technologies. Yet gate-tunable coherent control of multi-order and non-perturbative currents has remained elusive. Here, we demonstrate lightwave control over the amplitude, phase flips, and zero-crossings of THz field-driven high-order currents by utilizing phase-locked pairs of single-cycle THz fields in a dual-gated bilayer graphene (BLG) device. By leveraging the in-situ tuning of both bandgap and charge doping, we reveal extreme THz photocurrent rectification and sub-THz-cycle current generation that are governed by a strong field-driven crossover from interband multi-photon coherence to intraband lightwave acceleration, whose competition provides an exceptional sensitivity to the THz-pulse pair drive. Our interferometric nonlinear THz photocurrent spectroscopy of sub-cycle electronic excitations accesses and controls non-perturbative, multi-quantum photocurrents, which manifest as an extreme-order THz frequency comb. Quantum-kinetic simulations map these lightwave-controlled electron trajectories, revealing that sub-THz-cycle ballistic transport underpins gate- and THz-field-driven functionality enabled by electrostatic doping and bandgap tuning within the atomically thin device architecture. The demonstrated on-demand coherent control of nonperturbative photocurrent transport regimes can be extended to a broad range of atomically thin device architectures, opening new avenues for gate- and field-programmable logic, memory, and quantum simulators in which electronic pathways are steered on sub-cycle, petahertz timescales and atomic length scales.
