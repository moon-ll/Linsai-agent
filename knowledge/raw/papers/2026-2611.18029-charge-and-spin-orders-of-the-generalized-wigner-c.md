# Charge and spin orders of the generalized Wigner crystal phases at fractional fillings in twisted MoTe$_2$: unrestricted Hartree-Fock and variational Monte Carlo studies

## 摘要

Recent experiments have revealed interaction-driven insulating states at various fractional fillings of twisted bilayer MoTe$_2$. Many of these insulating states are believed to be generalized Wigner crystals (GWC) condensed in the background of triangular moir$\acute{e}$ lattice. In this work, we focus on revealing the charge and spin order of the insulating states at 4/3 and 3/2 hole fillings over a range of twist angles and applied displacement fields using unrestricted Hartree-Fock (HF) and variational Monte Carlo (VMC) methods. We find that, at 4/3 filling for low twist angles the charge ordered ground state is valley-polarized (VP), characterized by weak charge delocalization approaching the classical limit. Application of the displacement field induces layer-polarization and suppresses the charge fluctuations even further. In contrast, at 3/2 filling, the VP phase exhibits signatures of a two-component GWC phase, which is particularly robust at finite displacement field. Additionally, by increasing the twist angle one pushes the system into an intervalley-coherent (IVC) phase, which is found to be fluctuation-dominated. Our VMC-based analysis shows that the correlation energy is negligible in the VP phase, which is consistent with its classical nature. However, the IVC phase shows significant gain in correlation energy, which is dominated by the nonlocal correlations captured by the Jastrow factors, while the local Gutzwiller factors play a minor role.
