# Magnetized plasma modulator for polarization-tunable ultrahigh-power single-cycle lasers

## 摘要

Ultrahigh power lasers with tunable polarization and few-cycle duration have long been considered the holy grail of high-power laser science, as they push light-matter interactions into an entirely new physical regime. Yet such pulses remain far beyond the reach of conventional solid-state optics and technologies. Here, we introduce a novel magnetized plasma modulator that boosts linearly polarized lasers from petawatt to exawatt powers while simultaneously enabling tunable polarization, offering a promising approach for high-power laser development. This is achieved in density-tailored plasmas by combining dispersive compression of a chirped picosecond laser pulse with magneto-chiral group-velocity splitting in a few-Tesla magnetic field. It can generate circularly or elliptically polarized pulses with extremely high compression ratios (over 1000) and extremely short durations (down to the single-cycle level) in a compact and efficient manner. These findings demonstrate a feasible pathway toward exawatt-class few-cycle lasers with tunable polarization, opening new avenues for a wide range of cutting-edge research and applications.
