# Two-dimensional ground-state mapping of a Mott-Hubbard system in a flexible field-effect device

Flexible EDL transistor on organic Mott insulator κ-(BEDT-TTF)2Cu[N(CN)2]Cl maps the first experimental2D ground-state phase diagram of a Hubbard system by simultaneous bandfilling (gate) and bandwidth (strain) tuning, revealing a superconducting dome around the Mott tip.
