# Charge and spin orders of the generalized Wigner crystal phases at fractional fillings in twisted MoTe2

## 摘要
Recent experiments have revealed interaction-driven insulating states at various fractional fillings of twisted bilayer MoTe2. Many of these insulating states are believed to be generalized Wigner crystals (GWC) condensed in the background of triangular moire lattice. In this work, we focus on revealing the charge and spin order of the insulating states at 4/3 and 3/2 hole fillings over a range of twist angles and applied displacement fields using unrestricted Hartree-Fock (HF) and variational Monte Carlo (VMC) methods. We find that, at 4/3 filling for low twist angles the charge ordered ground state is valley-polarized (VP), characterized by weak charge delocalization approaching the classical limit. Application of the displacement field induces layer-polarization and suppresses the charge fluctuations even further. In contrast, at 3/2 filling, the VP phase exhibits signatures of a two-component GWC phase, which is particularly robust at finite displacement field. Additionally, by increasing the twist angle one pushes the system into an intervalley-coherent (IVC) phase, which is found to be fluctuation-dominated. Our VMC-based analysis shows that the correlation energy is negligible in the VP phase, which is consistent with its classical nature. However, the IVC phase shows significant gain in correlation energy, which is dominated by the nonlocal correlations captured by the Jastrow factors, while the local Gutzwiller factors play a minor role.

## 背景概述与问题引入
扭转双层 MoTe2 在分数空穴填充下出现一系列相互作用驱动的绝缘态，普遍被理解为三角莫尔格子背景上的广义 Wigner 晶体（GWC），但其电荷与自旋序的微观结构——尤其在不同扭转角与位移场下的演化——尚缺系统刻画。本文用非限制 Hartree-Fock 与变分蒙特卡洛双方法交叉验证 4/3 与 3/2 填充的基态相图。

## 关键发现及核心讨论

- **4/3 填充：谷极化 GWC 趋经典**：低扭转角下电荷序基态为谷极化（VP），电荷离域弱、接近经典极限；位移场诱导层极化并进一步压制电荷涨落
- **3/2 填充：双分量 GWC**：VP 相呈现双分量 GWC 特征，且在有限位移场下特别稳定——填充与位移场共同决定晶体组分数
- **扭转角推向谷间相干（IVC）**：增大扭转角使体系进入涨落主导的 IVC 相；VMC 分析显示 VP 相关联能可忽略（与其经典性一致），而 IVC 相关联能增益显著且由 Jastrow 因子捕捉的非局域关联主导，局域 Gutzwiller 因子贡献很小

## 主要方法
理论计算：非限制 Hartree-Fock + 变分蒙特卡洛（VMC，含 Gutzwiller 局域与 Jastrow 非局域变分因子），扫描扭转角与垂直位移场两个轴，比较 4/3 与 3/2 空穴填充下的 VP/层极化/IVC 序与关联能分解。

## 创新性
用 HF 与 VMC 双方法对同一莫尔体系做关联能分解（Gutzwiller vs Jastrow），定量区分 VP 相的经典性与 IVC 相的非局域关联本质；明确了"扭转角—位移场—填充"三维相图的边界。

## 结论与展望
绘制了 tMoTe2 分数填充 GWC 的谷极化-层极化-谷间相干相图；对超快方向的提示：谷极化与谷间相干态具有不同的电荷离域度与关联结构，光场诱导的 VP↔IVC 转变（如光泵浦谷间相干调控）可能产生特征性的超快电荷/自旋响应信号。
