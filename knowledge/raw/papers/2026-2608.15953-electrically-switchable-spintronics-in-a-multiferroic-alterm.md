# Electrically Switchable Spintronics in a Multiferroic Altermagnet

We introduce a minimal model of a two-dimensional lattice that, upon spontaneous symmetry breaking, simultaneously develops altermagnetic order, a finite electric polarization, and a spin-polarized transport response, all of which are controlled by an external electric field.
