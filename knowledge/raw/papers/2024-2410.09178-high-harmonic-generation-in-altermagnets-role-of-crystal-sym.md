# High harmonic generation in altermagnets: role of crystal symmetry and spin ordering

Detailed first-principles and symmetry-based investigation of HHG in representative altermagnetic candidates (RuO2, MnTe, CrSb), quantifying the harmonic yield, ellipticity dependence, and spin polarization of emitted radiation across different altermagnetic spin-symmetry classes.
