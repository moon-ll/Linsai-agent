# Inverse Problems in High-Harmonic Generation: A Rank-Deficiency Perspective on Attosecond Spectroscopy

Analyzes the rank-deficient structure of HHG retrieval problems, showing that effective parameter dimension grows logarithmically with harmonic order and proposing truncated-SVD regularization with optimal rank selection for attosecond streaking/tomography inversions.
