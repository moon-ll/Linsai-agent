# Failed superconductivity in a Mott spin liquid material

Magnetotransport and ac susceptibility on chemically substituted κ-STFx organic Mott spin liquids show global superconductivity never sets in even as T→0; superconducting domains embedded in percolating metal undergo a quantum superconductor-to-metal transition with universal conductance fluctuations at high field.
