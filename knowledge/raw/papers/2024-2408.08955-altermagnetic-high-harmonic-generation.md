# Altermagnetic high harmonic generation

Theoretical prediction that altermagnets can generate high harmonics from linearly polarized driving fields, exhibiting a unique spin-dependent spectral structure that distinguishes them from ferromagnets and antiferromagnets. The work establishes altermagnets as a new platform for ultrafast spintronics and nonlinear optics.
