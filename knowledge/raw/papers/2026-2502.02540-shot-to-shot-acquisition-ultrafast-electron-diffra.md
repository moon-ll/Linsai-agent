# Shot-to-shot acquisition ultrafast electron diffraction

**arXiv**: [2502.02540](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02540)
**作者**: Rémi Claude, Michele Puppin, Bruce Weaver
**方向**: 强场非平衡态动力学
**deepxiv 评分**: 0.16666667

## TL;DR

[experimental physics research paper] A shot-to-shot acquisition method is implemented in ultrafast electron diffraction (UED) at 20 kHz repetition rate using phase-locked synchronization and a direct electron detector, enabling sub-picosecond time resolution and a one-order-of-magnitude improvement in signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) by leveraging low electron flux (<1000 electrons/pulse) and noise-minimized acquisition. The method achieves noise levels as low as 10⁻⁴ through pump-probe data normalization using unpumped frames, overcoming limitations of traditional high-flux approaches that suffer from space-charge broadening, and demonstrates feasibility in real-world transient dynamics studies of materials like graphite under optical excitation.

## 摘要

We demonstrate a novel shot-to-shot acquisition method for optical pump - keV electron energy probe in ultrafast scattering experiments. We integrate a phase-locked acquisition scheme at a repetition rate of 20kHz in a conventional ultrafast electron diffraction (UED) setup. We proceed to a full characterization of the noise level in different configurations and for realistic scenarios. The shot-to-shot acquisition improves the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) by one order of magnitude and can be readily implemented in other high-repetition rate electron diffraction and spectroscopy setups.
