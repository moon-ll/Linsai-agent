# Quantum-Metric-Induced Distinct Optoelectronic Responses in Triple $M$ Valleys

## 摘要

While $K_{\pm}$ valleys with contrasting optoelectronic properties have been the main focus of valleytronics, the lack of reliable manipulation methods for $M$ valleys leaves numerous $M$-valley materials untapped. Here, we discover that three flavors of $M$ valleys become distinguishable via quantum-metric-induced optoelectronic responses. The tight-binding calculations demonstrate that by tuning the electric-field orientation, opposite nonlinear Hall currents from any pair of $M$ valleys can be chosen to realize the nonlinear valley Hall effect. Moreover, linearly polarized light allows for preferential excitation of individual $M$ valleys, driving photoexcited valley-polarized Hall transport. Using first-principles calculations, we further validate the quantum-metric-induced optoelectronic responses in antiferromagnetic $\mathrm{MoI}_3$ bilayers, which exhibit a significant nonlinear valley Hall conductivity amenable to transport measurements. Our findings extend the scope of valleytronics from conventional binary $K_{\pm}$ valleys to triple $M$ valleys and pave the way for designing advanced optoelectronic devices with multiple quantum states of high tunability.
