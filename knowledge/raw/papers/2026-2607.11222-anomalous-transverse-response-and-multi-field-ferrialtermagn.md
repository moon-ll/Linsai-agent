# Anomalous Transverse Response and Multi-Field Ferrialtermagnetic-Ferroelectric Valve with CrSb Flakes

DFT + MC simulations + NEGF of 3-atomic-layer CrSb(110) flakes show alternating spin splitting of 344 meV, moderate MAE, TN=657 K, and MOKE/AHE, enabling multi-field ferrialtermagnetic-ferroelectric valve functionality.
