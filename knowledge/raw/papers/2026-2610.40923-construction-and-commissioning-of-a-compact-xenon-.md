# Construction and Commissioning of a Compact Xenon Gas Recycling System for a High-Harmonic Generation VUV Frequency Comb

**arXiv**: [2610.40923](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40923)
**作者**: G. Holthoff, J. Rebhan, T. Teuner
**方向**: HHG光谱学
**deepxiv 评分**: 0.5

## TL;DR

A compact, closed-loop xenon recycling system for application in a High-Harmonic Generation (HHG) Vacuum-UV (VUV) frequency comb laser systems is designed, constructed and commissioned.

## 摘要

A compact, closed-loop xenon recycling system for application in a High-Harmonic Generation (HHG) Vacuum-UV (VUV) frequency comb laser systems is designed, constructed and commissioned. It consists of three main sections, a low-pressure section (LPS) pre-filtering the gas of particulates, water and hydrocarbons, then a compression station (CS), allowing for a variable pressure of up to 60 bar, and a high-pressure section (HPS) with more filtering, pressure control and monitoring. The system proves to meet all design criteria in gas quality (the sum off all hydrocarbon contaminations up to 120 amu and CO$_{2}$ being below 10$^{-8}$ mbar) and recovery efficiency (>99.8$\%$ for all expected operational cases, neglecting possible geometric gas catcher losses in the HHG laser itself). The operation and results are presented and discussed. Furthermore, the system is compared to the performance of a similar system from which it is derived.
