# Energy conservation in quantum optical high harmonic generation

**arXiv**: [2410.15503](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.15503)
**作者**: Philipp Stammer
**方向**: 固体HHG
**deepxiv 评分**: 0.2

## TL;DR

[research paper] The paper introduces a quantum optical framework for high harmonic generation (HHG) by defining an 'energy conserving subspace' that formally enforces photon number conservation across driving and harmonic modes. This subspace naturally leads to entangled photonic states with non-classical marginals, resolving a long-standing gap in quantum descriptions of HHG where energy conservation was previously unaccounted for in quantum state formalisms. The approach explains recent experimental results on conditioned HHG yielding high-photon-number optical cat states through energy-conserving photon exchanges, without requiring post-selection or additional assumptions.

## 摘要

The use of energy conservation arguments is ubiquitous in understanding the process of high harmonic generation, yet a complete quantum optical description of photon exchange remained elusive. Here, we solve this gap in description by introducing the energy conserving subspace in high harmonic generation in which many photons of the driving field are absorbed to generate a single photon of higher energy. The presented solution to energy conservation in quantum optical high harmonic generation naturally results in highly entangled states of light with non-classical properties in their marginals. This further allows to explain recent experimental results for quantum state engineering, for which we provide analytical bounds on the fidelity with a high photon number optical cat state.
