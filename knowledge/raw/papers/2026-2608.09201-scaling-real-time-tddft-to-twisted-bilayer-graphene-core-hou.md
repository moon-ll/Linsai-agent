# Scaling Real-Time TDDFT to Twisted Bilayer Graphene: Core-Hour Benchmarks and Hybrid Tight-Binding Acceleration

Benchmarks rt-TDDFT and a TDDFT/TB hybrid on commensurate and incommensurate TBG supercells, reporting core-hour scaling with system size, twist angle, and basis set, and proposing a multiscale embedding for moiré-scale electronic dynamics.
