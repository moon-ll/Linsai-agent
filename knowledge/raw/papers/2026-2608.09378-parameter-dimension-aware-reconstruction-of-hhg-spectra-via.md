# Parameter-Dimension-Aware Reconstruction of HHG Spectra via Bayesian Inference

Applies Bayesian uncertainty quantification to the HHG inverse problem, identifying the effective parameter dimension from posterior correlations and showing robust reconstruction when harmonic counts exceed the rank of the dipole response matrix.
