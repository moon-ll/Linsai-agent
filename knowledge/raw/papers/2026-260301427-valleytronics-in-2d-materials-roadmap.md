# Valleytronics in 2D Materials Roadmap

## 摘要
Valleytronics exploits non-equivalent energy extrema in the electronic band structure of crystalline solids to encode, manipulate, and read out information. The advent of 2D materials made valley control practical through optical, electrical, and magnetic routes. This foundation has enabled remarkable progress spanning established frontiers, such as valley exciton physics and valley Hall effects, as well as emerging directions including lightwave valleytronics, nanophotonic integration, flat-band valleytronics, and spin-valley qubits. This Roadmap brings together perspectives from leading experts to chart the key opportunities and challenges at the forefront of 2D material valleytronics.

## 背景概述与问题引入
能谷电子学利用布洛赫带非等价能量极值编码信息。2D 材料（石墨烯、TMD）使谷控制通过光学/电学/磁学路线实用化。本路线图汇集领域顶尖专家，梳理既有前沿（谷激子物理、谷霍尔效应）与新兴方向（光波谷电子学、纳米光子集成、平带谷电子学、自旋-谷量子比特），并识别关键开放挑战。

## 关键发现及核心讨论
1. **领域版图梳理**：确立谷激子物理、谷霍尔效应为成熟前沿；光波谷电子学（lightwave valleytronics）、平带谷电子学、自旋-谷量子比特为新兴增长点。
2. **关键开放挑战**：可扩展性（材料规模化）、TMD/石墨烯之外的新谷平台、与纳米光子学和非线性光学控制的集成。
3. **物理基础重申**：Berry 曲率、谷极化、量子几何是实现谷信息处理的核心资源。
