# Berry Curvature-Driven Circular High-Harmonic Generation in Sliding Ferroelectric Bilayers

TDDFT simulations predict that the broken inversion symmetry of sliding ferroelectric bilayers produces strong circularly polarized high harmonics whose helicity directly maps the layer-locked Berry curvature distribution, providing a contact-free optical readout of ferroelectric polarization.
