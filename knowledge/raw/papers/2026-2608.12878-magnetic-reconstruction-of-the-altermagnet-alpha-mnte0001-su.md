# Magnetic reconstruction of the altermagnet alpha-MnTe(0001) surface driven by ligand holes

We show from first principles that the altermagnet alpha-MnTe reconstructs its magnetic order at the (0001) surface, into a stacking with a ferromagnetic outermost Mn bilayer that is lower in energy than the bulk-continued one.
