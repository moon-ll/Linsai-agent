# Momentum-Space Mismatch Enables Non-Hermitian Valley Filtering

**arXiv**: [2611.15036](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15036)
**作者**: Kai Shao
**方向**: 能谷电子学

## 摘要

Valley-selective transport is central to valleytronics, but existing valley filters are largely tailored to specific materials or lattice geometries, motivating the search for a common microscopic ingredient. Here we identify the momentum-space mismatch between the band-touching nodes of a system and its dissipative reservoir as a route to node-selective non-Hermitian skin effects (NHSE). When the two are coupled, the mismatch is encoded in the retarded self-energy, producing node-dependent dissipation and contrasting quasiparticle lifetimes. The resulting effective non-Hermitian Hamiltonian exhibits opposite point-gap spectral winding at the two nodes, giving rise to a node-resolved NHSE with strongly asymmetric dissipation rates: one node decays rapidly into the reservoir while the other survives over the device, yielding a nearly valley-polarized outgoing current. We demonstrate this mechanism in a minimal one-dimensional dimer chain and in two-dimensional systems hosting genuine Dirac valleys, including $π$-flux rectangular and honeycomb lattices. In all cases, transport calculations yield nearly 100\% valley polarization, while remaining gate-tunable and robust against disorder and edge imperfections. Our results show that momentum-space mismatch provides a route to non-Hermitian valley filtering.
