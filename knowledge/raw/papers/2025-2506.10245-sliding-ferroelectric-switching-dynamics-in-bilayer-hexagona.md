# Sliding Ferroelectric Switching Dynamics in Bilayer Hexagonal Boron Nitride under Applied Field

Time-resolved Kelvin probe force microscopy quantifies the switching time of sliding ferroelectricity in bilayer hBN devices, finding field-dependent transition kinetics on the order of nanoseconds with implications for low-power ferroelectric memory applications.
