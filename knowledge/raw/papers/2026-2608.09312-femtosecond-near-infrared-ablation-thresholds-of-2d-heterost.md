# Femtosecond Near-Infrared Ablation Thresholds of 2D Heterostructures: h-BN Encapsulated Graphene and TMD Monolayers

Experimental and theoretical determination of single-pulse NIR (800–1550 nm) ablation thresholds for h-BN-encapsulated graphene, MoS2, and MoSe2, quantifying damage fluence as a function of layer count, encapsulation, and substrate, with relevance for attosecond-driven device processing.
