# Electric Fields Turn Vacancy-trapping Grain Boundaries into Li-Ion Pathways in Li2O

**arXiv**: [2611.17679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17679)
**作者**: Youjeong Choi, Po-yen Chem, Tasuku Sugiura
**方向**: 强场非平衡态动力学
**deepxiv 评分**: 0.5

## TL;DR

Ion transport in solids has conventionally been understood from equilibrium diffusion, yet ions in batteries are driven by electric fields. Does field-driven ion transport follow equilibrium behavior?

## 摘要

Ion transport in solids has conventionally been understood from equilibrium diffusion, yet ions in batteries are driven by electric fields. Does field-driven ion transport follow equilibrium behavior? We examined this question in bulk Li2O and at its grain boundaries (GBs) using electric-field molecular dynamics combining a machine learning interatomic potential with a Bader charge prediction model. Although Li diffusion in cubic Li2O is isotropic at zero field, an electric field renders it orientation dependent because Li hops along a limited set of crystallographic pathways that the field biases unequally. At GBs, this anisotropy becomes a qualitative difference: a GB that strongly traps Li vacancies but exhibits a strong field response releases them under a field, whereas a weakly responsive GB retains them despite weaker vacancy trapping. These results show that field-driven GB transport is governed not by static thermodynamics alone but by the angle between the electric field and the diffusion pathways of the constituent grains.
