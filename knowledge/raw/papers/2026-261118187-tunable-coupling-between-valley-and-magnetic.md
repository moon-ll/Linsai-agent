# Tunable coupling between valley and magnetic excitons in WS2/CrSBr heterostructures

## 摘要
Interfacial coupling in van der Waals two-dimensional (2D) heterostructures offers an effective route to tailor the electronic, optical, and magnetic properties of materials. Versatile control and precise engineering of such interlayer interactions can be further achieved through external parameters, like electric field, magnetic field, optical excitation, as well as layer stacking geometries. Here, we examined the magneto-optical properties of 2D semiconductor heterostructures made of antiferromagnetic CrSBr and a monolayer WS2, both of which host intriguing excitonic properties. We identified a type-II band alignment between these two layers and the magnetic proximity effects from CrSBr, manifested by an enhanced Zeeman valley splitting in the WS2 layer. The interlayer charge transfer shows strong spin selectivity and is governed by the relative alignment between the valley-spin polarization of photoexcited carriers in WS2 and the magnetization in CrSBr, giving rise to spin-sensitive circularly polarized photoluminescence (PL) from WS2. Furthermore, the highly anisotropic electronic dispersion in CrSBr enables stacking-dependent control of interlayer interactions, as inferred from a twist-angle dependence of PL quenching. Our work establishes a highly tunable 2D system that integrates valleytronics and magnetic excitons through multiple coupling channels, opening up new opportunities in all-optical manipulation of spin and magnon states and for the development of flexible spin-optoelectronic devices.

## 背景概述与问题引入
能谷电子学与磁性激子是二维材料中两条并行的操控自由度，但把二者耦合到同一个可调体系中一直缺乏干净的实现平台。反铁磁半导体 CrSBr 具有强各向异性激子与近邻磁性，单层 WS2 则是标准谷-自旋锁定体系——本文的问题是：把两者叠成异质结后，光激发的谷极化载流子与反铁磁磁化之间能否建立可测、可调的耦合通道。

## 关键发现及核心讨论

- **II 型能带对齐 + 磁近邻增强 Zeeman 谷劈裂**：磁光测量确认 WS2/CrSBr 为 II 型对齐，CrSBr 的磁近邻效应直接表现为 WS2 层 Zeeman 谷劈裂增强——谷自由度被相邻反铁磁层改写
- **自旋选择的层间电荷转移**：层间电荷转移强度由 WS2 光激发载流子的谷-自旋极化与 CrSBr 磁化的相对取向决定，产生对自旋敏感的圆偏振 PL——这是一个"谷极化读磁化"的光学通道
- **转角依赖的 PL 猝灭**：CrSBr 高度各向异性的电子色散使层间相互作用随堆叠角变化，PL 猝灭的转角依赖提供了堆叠调控耦合的证据

## 主要方法
实验：单层 WS2 / 反铁磁 CrSBr 范德华异质结的磁光光谱（变磁场、变偏振、变转角 PL 与磁圆二色），提取 Zeeman 谷劈裂、圆偏振 PL 磁控行为与转角依赖的 PL 猝灭，判定能带对齐类型与磁近邻效应。

## 创新性
在单一范德华平台内同时接入谷电子学与磁性激子两条通道，且耦合可经由磁场、光激发、转角三路调节；此前谷-磁耦合多以磁性掺杂或大磁场实现，本体系无需掺杂、界面干净。

## 结论与展望
建立了 WS2/CrSBr 这一高度可调的谷-磁耦合平台，指明全光操控自旋与磁振子态及柔性自旋光电子器件方向；后续自然的问题包括超快时间分辨下这一自旋选择层间转移的动力学时标，以及磁序翻转对谷极化的瞬态调控——正是光场驱动超快实验可介入的缺口。
