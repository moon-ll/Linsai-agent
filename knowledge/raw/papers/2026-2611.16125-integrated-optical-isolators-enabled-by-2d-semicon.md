# Integrated optical isolators enabled by 2D semiconductors via spin-momentum locking

## 摘要

Integrated optical isolators based on spin-momentum locking hold great potential for protecting photonic integrated circuits from unwanted reflections. Currently, the most common implementations are magneto-optical isolators that require an externally applied magnetic field. Nevertheless, a wide range of nonreciprocal effects remains to be exploited in alternative material platforms. Although the majority of these effects induce changes to the propagation of light as a function of its polarization, they can be directly connected to the propagation direction through spin-momentum locking. We employ a computational approach to investigate various designs of integrated isolators based on nonreciprocal 2D semiconductors, studying the influence of their geometry and optical properties on the device performance. Additionally, we propose a novel concept for an all-optical isolator based on spin-valley polarization and optically tunable magnetism in transition metal dichalcogenide monolayers. Our results establish 2D semiconductors as an advanced platform for integrated nonreciprocal devices, enabling functionalities that are difficult to achieve with conventional magneto-optical materials like all-optical operation.
