# Coherent Multidimensional Spectroscopy at the Few-Cycle Limit: Accounting for the Pump Spectrum

## 摘要
Coherent multidimensional spectroscopy will benefit greatly from near single-cycle pulses through the spectral breadth of states that can be accessed and the high frequency oscillations that can be resolved. However, the necessary cost of working with such pulses is the spectral structure arising from efficient self-phase modulation, which imparts into the signal and complicates interpretation of the molecular response. In this paper, we develop an in-operando approach to characterize and remove pump spectral features from two-dimensional spectroscopic signals. The pump spectrum is retrieved via an interferometric integrated autocorrelation measurement that runs concurrently with the experiment and thereby inherits the same time and frequency domain properties. A deconvolution procedure then suppresses noise and extracts the third-order response from the total signal. The analysis enables correction of spectral intensities and unambiguous assignment of spectral features to the molecular response. This work pushes multidimensional spectroscopy toward the impulsive limit and enables integration with other nonlinear spectroscopies such as attosecond spectroscopy - all without compromising few-cycle pulse properties.

## 背景概述与问题引入

相干多维光谱（CMDS）若用近单周期脉冲驱动，将同时获得宽谱态覆盖与高频振荡分辨能力。代价是：高效自相位调制（SPM）给泵浦脉冲带入光谱结构，污染二维谱信号，使分子响应的指认复杂化。

## 关键发现及核心讨论

- **in-operando 泵浦谱表征**：用与实验并发的干涉式积分自相关测量实时检索泵浦谱——继承与实验完全相同的时域/频域特性，无需独立标定。
- **去卷积提取三阶响应**：去卷积程序抑制噪声并从总信号中分离出三阶响应，实现谱强度修正与谱特征对分子响应的无歧义指认。

## 主要方法

- 干涉式积分自相关（interferometric integrated autocorrelation）在线测量
- 去卷积信号处理提取三阶非线性响应
- few-cycle 泵浦的二维光谱实验

## 创新性

在不牺牲 few-cycle 脉冲特性的前提下修正泵浦光谱伪影，把多维光谱推向 impulsive 极限，并打通与阿秒光谱等其它非线性光谱的整合路径。

## 结论与展望

方法论工具性质的工作：任何用 few-cycle/单周期脉冲做相位敏感测量的实验（包括 HHG 光谱学中的驱动场表征）都面临相同的 SPM 光谱结构问题，此 in-operando 修正思路可直接迁移。
