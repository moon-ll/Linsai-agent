# Valley Berry curvature dipole induced nonlinear valley Hall effect

## 摘要
Valley Hall effect is a signature effect in the field of valleytronics, and recent studies have pushed this effect into the nonlinear regime. Here, we reveal a previously unexplored type of nonlinear valley Hall effect which arises from a valley Berry curvature dipole (vBCD) mechanism. We show this nonlinear valley Hall effect is forbidden for conventional time-reversal-connected valleys, but is supported in the class of valleytronic systems featuring time-reversal-invariant valleys. The candidate layer groups and detailed symmetry constraints on vBCD are obtained. It shows the nonlinear valley Hall response, as well as the nonlinear charge Hall response, can be well controlled by tuning the driving field direction. We demonstrate our proposal in an effective model study and in a concrete material example, strained Nb3SBr7, by first-principles calculations. The nonlocal transport signature of this vBCD induced nonlinear valley Hall effect is also discussed.

## 背景概述与问题引入

能谷霍尔效应（valley Hall effect）是能谷电子学的标志性效应，近期研究将其推入非线性区。非线性霍尔效应的已知机制（Berry 曲率偶极 BCD）在能谷体系中如何呈现、受何种对称性约束，是本文要回答的问题。

## 关键发现及核心讨论

- **vBCD 机制**：提出 valley Berry curvature dipole（vBCD）诱导的一类新型非线性能谷霍尔效应。
- **对称性判据**：该效应对常规时间反演连接（time-reversal-connected）的能谷禁止，但在具有时间反演不变能谷的能谷电子体系中允许——给出候选层群与详细对称性约束。
- **场方向调控**：非线性能谷霍尔响应与非线性电荷霍尔响应均可通过驱动场方向调控。
- **材料实例**：应变 Nb3SBr7 的第一性原理计算验证提案，并讨论非局域输运特征。

## 主要方法

- 对称性分析（层群分类 + vBCD 约束推导）
- 有效模型研究
- 第一性原理计算（应变 Nb3SBr7）

## 创新性

识别出一类被对称性选择定则保护的全新非线性霍尔通道，把 Berry 曲率偶极概念推广到能谷分辨的版本。

## 结论与展望

属 DC 输运方向的非线性响应理论。对林赛组的价值在机理对照：vBCD 的能谷分辨非线性响应框架可与光场驱动的能谷非线性光学（如 valley-HHG 的圆二色响应）做对称性语言上的同构比较——哪些对称性约束在 DC 输运与光驱动两个通道中一致，是可深挖的问题。
