# Near single-cycle pulse generation using a cascaded all-bulk multi-pass cell compression system

## 摘要
We experimentally demonstrate the generation of sub-two-cycle optical pulses using a cascaded post-compression scheme based on two bulk multi-pass cells. Starting from 220 fs, 100 $μ$J pulses around 1030 nm, sequential spectral broadening and pulse compression reduce the pulse duration first to 50 fs and ultimately to 5.5 fs (1.6 optical cycles) at a central wavelength of 1058 nm. The ultra-broadband spectrum is enabled by a dispersion-controlled cavity. Comprehensive spectral, temporal, and spatial characterization confirms excellent pulse quality. Despite operating the second multi-pass cell at peak powers several hundred times above the critical power for self-focusing in fused silica, no significant spatio-spectral or spatio-temporal distortions are observed, enabling direct use of the compressed 40 $μ$J pulses in further experiments. Numerical simulations of the nonlinear spectral broadening show excellent agreement with the experimental results, supporting the underlying physical picture. These findings establish bulk multi-pass cells as an efficient, compact, and robust platform for generating few-cycle pulses with excellent beam quality, offering considerable potential for strong-field and ultrafast applications, including extreme-ultraviolet generation and isolated attosecond pulse production.

## 背景概述与问题引入
强场与阿秒实验需要兼具高峰值功率、宽光谱和良好空间质量的少周期驱动脉冲。传统后压缩方案往往在能量扩展、稳定性与紧凑性之间存在权衡。本文考察级联全体材料多程池能否把百微焦脉冲压缩到接近单周期，同时避免高功率传播引入的时空畸变。

## 关键发现及核心讨论
- **发现 1：** 输入为中心波长约 1030 nm、脉宽 220 fs、能量 100 微焦的脉冲；两级展宽与压缩先将脉宽降至 50 fs，最终获得 5.5 fs 脉冲。
- **发现 2：** 输出中心波长为 1058 nm，5.5 fs 对应约 1.6 个光学周期；可直接用于后续实验的压缩脉冲能量为 40 微焦。
- **发现 3：** 第二级多程池的峰值功率达到熔融石英自聚焦临界功率的数百倍，但完整时域、频域与空间表征未发现显著时空或空谱畸变。
- **发现 4：** 非线性光谱展宽模拟与实验结果高度一致，为该高功率传播机制提供了数值支持。

## 主要方法
实验采用两个级联的全体材料多程池，依次完成非线性光谱展宽和色散补偿。第二级使用色散受控腔体产生超宽带光谱，并通过光谱、时间和空间诊断评估脉冲及光束质量；同时数值模拟材料中的非线性传播与光谱展宽。

## 创新性
把两级体材料多程池扩展到 1.6 周期、40 微焦的工作区间，并实验证明即使远高于材料自聚焦临界功率，仍能保持可用的时空质量。

## 结论与展望
该系统提供了紧凑、稳健且高效率的近单周期脉冲源。其输出可作为强场实验、极紫外产生和孤立阿秒脉冲产生的驱动光源。
