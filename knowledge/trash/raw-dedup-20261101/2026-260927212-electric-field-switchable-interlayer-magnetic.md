# Electric-field switchable interlayer magnetic order and anomalous valley Hall effect in Janus VSSe bilayers with different interfaces

## 摘要
Electric-field control of magnetic order and valley polarization holds great promise for spintronic and valleytronic applications. However, achieving such electrical modulation remains a fundamental challenge in two-dimensional (2D) van der Waals (vdW) magnets. Herein, via first-principles calculations, we verify that electrically tunable interlayer magnetic order and valley polarization can be realized in Janus VSSe bilayers with different interfaces. Although the magnitudes of dipole moments within each constituent Janus monolayer are identical, the vertical built-in electrostatic potential difference across the bilayer depends strongly on the orientations of these dipoles, which originates from the distinct interfacial configurations. As a consequence, the VSSe bilayers with different interfaces possess distinct interlayer magnetic couplings and show dramatically varied responses to external electric fields. For Se-S interface, reversible electric-field switching between antiferromagnetic (AFM) and ferromagnetic (FM) states can be achieved due to the competition between itinerant-electron-mediated FM exchange coupling and interlayer potential-difference-dependent AFM/FM super-superexchange interactions mediated by interfacial Se and S atoms. For Se-Se interface, both the valley polarization and spin splitting can be effectively reversed by the out-of-plane electric field.

## 背景概述与问题引入

二维范德华磁体中实现电场调控磁序与能谷极化，对自旋/能谷电子学应用意义重大，但电学调制仍是根本挑战。Janus 结构（面外镜破缺）带来内建偶极，为电场调控提供新自由度。

## 关键发现及核心讨论

- **界面构型决定物性**：Janus VSSe 双层中各单层偶极矩大小相同，但跨双层的内建静电势差强烈依赖偶极取向（即界面构型）——不同界面呈现不同层间磁耦合与电场响应。
- **Se-S 界面：AFM/FM 可逆切换**：巡游电子介导的 FM 交换与界面 Se/S 原子介导的势差依赖超-超交换竞争，实现电场可逆切换反铁磁/铁磁态。
- **Se-Se 界面：能谷极化反转**：面外电场可同时反转能谷极化与自旋劈裂。

## 主要方法

- 第一性原理计算（DFT）：不同界面构型的磁序、能谷结构、电场响应
- 交换机制分析：巡游电子 FM 交换 vs 超超交换 AFM 的竞争

## 创新性

用 Janus 偶极取向作为界面工程自由度，把磁序切换与能谷调控统一到同一个静电势差参量上。

## 结论与展望

静态物性设计类工作，无超快动力学内容。对林赛组的参考价值在材料侧：电场可切换磁序 + 能谷极化的 Janus 双层是潜在的光驱动非线性光学/HHG 候选体系（破缺对称性组合丰富），若后续有超快实验跟进值得关注。
