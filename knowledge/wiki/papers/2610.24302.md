## 深度解析

### 研究问题（Research Question）

RT-TDDFT study of even-order harmonic anisotropy in ferroelectric (2D layered) NbOI2, answering a question the ferroelectric-HHG line had left open: how tunable is the angular anisotropy across harmonic orders and wavelengths. Key finding: SHG stays locked to the ferroelectric polar axis through 2150-2450 nm driving, but the FHG preferred axis ROTATES with wavelength - driven by the interplay of transition-dipole matrix elements and interband excitations between very narrow (flat) bands near the Fermi level.

### 研究背景（Background）

Lands directly on the ferroelectric x strong-field intersection the program has been mapping (VSSe interface paper, ferroelectric brightening of dark excitons, ferroelectric NbOI2 here). Three hooks: (1) The order-split behavior (SHG locked to polar axis, FHG rotatable) is a new entry for the order-parameter spectroscopy program: different harmonic orders read different symmetry content of the same ferroelectric state - SHG reads the macroscopic polar axis, FHG reads the near-Fermi flat-band pathway structure. For sigma_solid: the even-order decomposition in a ferroelectric would test whether the action formalism captures the order-dependent pathway selection. (2) The flat-band interband mechanism connects to the mixed-phase silicon paper (2611.17516): both show harmonic-order-dependent microscopic sensitivity, but here the selector is band topology (narrow bands) rather than real-space order - the two papers together demonstrate that harmonic order is a general microscope-selector across momentum-space and real-space structure. (3) The wavelength-as-steering-knob result is the static version of waveform engineering: if frequency rotates the FHG axis, a chirped pulse sweeps the anisotropy within one shot - a lightwave-ferroelectric switch with no moving parts, adjacent to project D's dynamical symmetry-control philosophy.

### 研究方法（Methods）

Combined RT-TDDFT + transition-dipole-matrix analysis for order-resolved anisotropy attribution; narrow-band interband pathway identification

### 主要发现与核心结论（Main Results）

- Wavelength-tunable anisotropy split by order: SHG maximizes along the ferroelectric polar axis across the whole 2150-2450 nm range (inversion-symmetry-breaking axis dominates), while the FHG-maximizing polarization direction changes with wavelength - the fourth harmonic samples a different microscopic pathway than the second
- Mechanism: interplay of transition dipole matrix elements and interband excitations between very narrow bands near the Fermi level - flat valence band (highest, ARPES-confirmed ordering); direct gap 1.75 eV (HSE06-consistent)
- Band-symmetry separation of contributions: dispersion along the polar axis is symmetric under momentum inversion (time-reversal + C2 about polar axis), and the non-polar axis dispersion is nearly symmetric - intraband contributions to even-order harmonics nearly vanish, letting interband components dominate (clean separation of channels)
- Angle-resolved even-order HHG established as a probe of anisotropic strong-field dynamics; wavelength-tunable anisotropy proposed as an optical-switching mechanism (angle-dependent even harmonics steered by frequency)
- Dielectric tensor components along polar vs non-polar axes show the linear-response anisotropy backbone (excitonic effects not included in the calculation - flagged limitation)

### 潜在研究方向（Future Directions）

Experimental verification in 2D NbOI2 flakes (mid-IR tunable source); extension to other layered ferroelectrics; dynamical (time-dependent) anisotropy under simultaneous gate control of polarization domains
