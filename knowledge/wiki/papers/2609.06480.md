## 深度解析

### 研究问题（Research Question）

Adapts the Jaynes-Cummings model to bridge cavity QED with strong-field/attosecond physics: a two-level system driven by a strong low-frequency CLASSICAL field plus a few quantized cavity modes. Multi-photon resonances between Stark-shifted Floquet states generate harmonics of the classical driver that are highly non-classical; when two harmonic modes are both in the cavity, the non-classicality of the resonant harmonic entangles the two harmonic modes. Exact treatment within the few-mode JC assumption (no other approximations). Dedicated to J.H. Eberly.

### 研究背景（Background）

This is the mechanistic bridge paper for the bsv program's cavity frontier. Three hooks: (1) It supplies the exactly-solvable model that the 2611.15997 perspective called for - when the strategic map asks how quantum light changes our understanding of materials, this paper shows the minimal architecture where the answer is exactly computable: classical strong drive + quantized few modes + resonance. (2) The Stark-shifted Floquet resonance as the non-classicality switch is the same physics family as the Lamb-shift modulation paper (2609.27742): environment-induced level shifts gating harmonic content - one classical-environment (Lindblad bath), one quantum-environment (cavity modes); the pair defines the two limits of environment-quantumness for harmonic generation. For any cavity-embedded sigma_solid experiment, the resonance condition derived here is the design equation. (3) The harmonic-harmonic entanglement (H3 x H5) opens the question the program should own: in solid HHG, can different harmonic orders become entangled via shared band-transition pathways? The JC result shows the entanglement mechanism requires only resonant mediation - solids provide dense resonant pathway networks, suggesting solid-HHG harmonic entanglement is plausible and unexplored.

### 研究方法（Methods）

Strongly-driven JC model as exactly-solvable reference system for quantum-optical HHG; Floquet-state Stark-shift resonance condition as the design rule for non-classical harmonic generation

### 主要发现与核心结论（Main Results）

- Mechanism chain: classical strong drive dresses the two-level system into Floquet states with quasienergies following the non-resonant Stark shift; as intensity sweeps during the pulse, two dressed states cross an N-photon resonance; the resonant harmonic (e.g. N=3) then acquires highly non-classical features - non-classicality is linked to the multi-photon resonance in the laser-dressed system, and its degree is sensitive to detuning, driving intensity, and transition frequency
- Two-mode entanglement mechanism: with H3 and H5 both cavity-supported and H5 near-resonant, H5 develops as a superposition of two coherent states; the atom-mediated coupling term (-i*lambda*a3-dagger) makes the joint state |alpha3>|chi5> evolve into an UNFACTORABLE wavefunction - the two harmonics become entangled (Eq. 2)
- Exactness claim: apart from the few-discrete-modes JC assumption, the treatment has no further approximations - the non-classical correlations are not perturbative artifacts but exact few-mode dynamics
- Positioning in the field: cites the growing quantum-optical-HHG literature (Lewenstein et al. Schrodinger-cat generation, Yi et al. massively entangled bright states PRX 2025, de-la-Pena fully quantum HHG theory, Theidel experimental evidence of quantum-optical nature PRX Quantum 2024, sub-Poissonian statistics 2026) - the JC approach complements these with an exactly solvable model
- The classical drive's >1e13 photons vs the few quantized cavity modes makes the setup a clean classical-quantum interface: harmonics of a classical field inherit quantum correlations from the resonant quantized mode

### 潜在研究方向（Future Directions）

Circuit-QED or polaritonic-material implementations (few discrete modes + strong classical drive available in cavities); extension beyond two levels; connection to the entangled-HHG experimental program
