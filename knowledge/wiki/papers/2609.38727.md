## 深度解析

### 研究问题（Research Question）

Comprehensive review of HHG in optical metaphotonic structures - isolated resonant nanoparticles and dielectric metasurfaces. Maps the transition from bulk-crystal HHG to subwavelength resonant platforms where high-Q modes (especially quasi-BIC) enhance harmonics by orders of magnitude at substantially reduced excitation intensities. The review consolidates three resonance families (plasmonic, Mie, BIC) and identifies the open theory gap that matters for modeling.

### 研究背景（Background）

Positions the resonant-platform axis of solid HHG - the structural-engineering complement to the medium-engineering (time-varying media, 2610.35646) and driver-engineering (vortex sculpting, 2512.19412) axes. For project A: if sigma_solid physics is ever tested on a metasurface platform, the saddle-point actions must be evaluated in the resonant near-field (inhomogeneous in space and phase) rather than a plane-wave drive - a substantial extension, but one that could amplify Berry-connection signatures via field-gradient effects. The review's identified theory gap (microscopic models compatible with resonant geometry) is exactly the kind of opening a semiclassical-action formulation is suited to fill: saddle-point trajectories are local, so they adapt to spatially varying drive fields more naturally than band-structure-free effective χ(n) models.

### 研究方法（Methods）

Review methodology - taxonomy of resonant metaphotonic HHG platforms with their resonance types, materials (Si, GaP, GaAs, ZnO nanowires, perovskites), demonstrated harmonic orders, and enhancement mechanisms; quasi-BIC symmetry-breaking design rules for nonlinear field confinement

### 主要发现与核心结论（Main Results）

- Three resonance families mapped for HHG enhancement: plasmonic (metal, field hotspots but damage/absorption limited), all-dielectric Mie resonances (higher damage threshold, anapole and magnetic modes), and quasi-BIC metasurfaces (highest Q, orders-of-magnitude enhancement but narrowband and symmetry-sensitive)
- Resonant metasurfaces enable HHG at pump intensities well below bulk-crystal thresholds - the field concentration does the work that peak intensity does in bulk
- Conversion efficiency for higher harmonics in metasurfaces still sits below conventional bulk systems - enhancement factors are large relative to unpatterned films of the same material but absolute yields remain the field's bottleneck
- Theory gap explicitly identified: microscopic descriptions and efficient numerical methods compatible with metasurface dimensions and propagation scales are urgently needed - most modeling imports bulk constitutive nonlinearities rather than treating the resonant near-field plus band structure self-consistently
- Temporal pulse design (optimal excursion without irreversible damage/heating) named as poorly explored control dimension on resonant platforms

### 潜在研究方向（Future Directions）

Self-consistent modeling coupling resonant near-fields to microscopic solid-HHG dynamics (the identified gap); broadband high-Q designs (multi-resonance and chirped BIC) to cover multiple harmonic orders; damage-threshold-optimized pulse temporal shaping on resonant platforms
