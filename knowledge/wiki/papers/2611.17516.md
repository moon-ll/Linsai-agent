# High-harmonic generation along a structural phase transition in silicon

**arXiv**: [2611.17516](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17516) | **方向**: 固体HHG

## 深度解析

### 研究问题（Research Question）

Experimental track of HHG across a continuously tunable crystal-to-amorphous phase transition, using Ga+ FIB dosing of silicon as the control knob. Four qualitatively distinct dose regimes are mapped; the mixed phase below critical dose shows harmonic-order-dependent sensitivity (low orders respond to amorphous pockets, plateau orders to residual crystal), and femtosecond laser annealing in the tunnel-ionization regime disentangles chemical from structural contributions. Positions HHG as a mesoscale structural probe of medium-range order.

### 研究背景（Background）

Direct hit on the order-parameter program: this is the structural-order analog of the CDW (TiSe2) and ferroelectric papers already in the program - here the order parameter is medium-range structural correlation itself, and HHG reads it order-by-order (harmonic order = lengthscale selector). For project A: the mixed-phase result quantifies when the fixed-band assumption degrades continuously - amorphous pockets break k-selection while residual medium-range order preserves partial anisotropy, giving a tunable dial between band-transport and real-space-hopping HHG limits. The annealing disentanglement (structure vs chemistry) is also a methodological template: any HHG-anomaly attribution should be checked against a structural-recovery control. Connects to 2505.02432 (pentacene - the weak-coupling molecular limit): silicon amorphization interpolates toward that limit continuously, and the two papers together bracket the order-axis of HHG spectroscopy.

### 研究方法（Methods）

FIB dose as continuous structural-order knob for HHG studies (amorphization without changing chemistry); harmonic-order-resolved structural probing (order parameter by correlation length); tunnel-ionization-regime laser annealing as an in-situ recovery tool

### 主要发现与核心结论（Main Results）

- Four dose regimes identified as Si transitions crystal-to-amorphous: below-critical mixed phase (spectra superpose crystalline and amorphous features), critical-dose giant enhancement of low-order harmonics (strongly correlated with AFM-measured surface swelling), high-dose quenching of high-order harmonics (disorder-induced dephasing), and very-high-dose damage-sensitivity regime correlated with FIB-induced etching
- Harmonic-order-as-lengthscale principle: h5 (low order) is sensitive to amorphous pockets while h11+ (plateau) remains crystalline-like until high dose - different harmonic orders probe different correlation lengths of the structural order, making the HHG spectrum a multi-scale order parameter
- Laser annealing disentanglement: intense non-resonant pulses preferentially deposit energy into amorphous regions (tunnel-ionization nonlinearity + higher defect density), recrystallizing them WITHOUT removing implanted Ga - the return to c-Si-like HHG after annealing proves structural modification (not chemical doping) is the dominant mechanism behind both the low-order enhancement and high-order quenching
- Polarization-resolved annealing signature: h5 polarization dependence is smeared in amorphized regions (residual medium-range order aligned to the original lattice gives slight anisotropy) and develops sharp c-Si peaks after annealing; h11 polarization structure is already crystalline before annealing - consistent with the mixed-phase order-asymmetry
- First demonstration of femtosecond laser annealing of a-Si in the tunnel-ionization regime (advantage over conventional thermal annealing: spatial selectivity to disordered regions, deep transmission of nonresonant IR)

### 潜在研究方向（Future Directions）

Extension to other FIB-amorphizable materials; time-resolved HHG during annealing (watching order recover in real time); quantitative inversion of harmonic spectra to medium-range-order correlation functions
