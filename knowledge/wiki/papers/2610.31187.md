## 深度解析

### 研究问题（Research Question）

Gap: HHG is predicted to create IR-XUV entanglement and squeezing, but XUV entanglement certification has no experimental protocol - standard schemes need mode-matched local oscillators or photon-number correlation detection across widely separated frequencies, both impractical in the XUV; no proven certification bounds existed for attosecond fields. Contributions: quantum-optical treatment of RABBIT showing its sidebands encode optical field correlation functions; a no-go theorem (single sideband can never certify entanglement); an NPT witness |Q|^2 > UV from the three-sideband dataset certifying IR-XUV entanglement; a sufficient GME witness |Q| > H(U,V) for genuine tripartite entanglement - all from electron detection alone.

### 研究背景（Background）

1) 直接对接我的量子光学驱动HHG方向: 我博后的原子-腔背景让我对'场的量子性质如何进入强场过程'敏感, 这篇给出的是认证端(verification)缺失的一环. 若我们做量子光驱动固体HHG, 下游自然要问'产生的场是否真的纠缠', 这套三边带RABBIT witness就是现成的读出协议, 且XUV段不需要本地振荡器 - 这是XUV量子计量最大的工程障碍. 2) 通道视角的结构同构: 他们把RABBIT边带拆成两条量子路径(a1+IR吸收 vs a2+IR发射)的干涉, 相干项Q是三模相干 <a2+ a1 a0^2>. 这与固体HHG里的通道分解(2611.16721的28通道)在结构上同构 - 都是'多通道相干 = 可观测的干涉量', 但一个在光子Fock空间, 一个在动量空间. 这个同构可以写进课题讨论: 通道相干性作为统一概念, 联结固体HHG偏振测量与量子光学认证. 3) 对Petz_nm/SBE编码的启示: Q含a0^2(IR双光子相干), U/V含粒子数关联 - 提示我在固体SBE框架里也应追踪密度矩阵的非对角元及其与驱动场涨落的耦合, 即腔/量子光驱动情形下rho_nm与场算符的联合演化, 这是把SBE量子化的自然入口(de-la-Pena 2025的二量子化数值模拟是同方向). 4) 实验设计注意: U = 0的光谱整形方案能把判据推到充要, 但需要光栅/单色器遮蔽相邻谐波 - 若在固体HHG上做类似认证, 谐波线宽和固体相位匹配可能让U_dc, V_dc上界松化, 认证区域会缩水, 这是可行性层面的实际约束. 5) 文献职责边界: 这篇是纯理论论文(单作者, ICFO), 给出的是协议与界, 没有实验演示 - 引用时应作为'认证协议已建立'而非'纠缠已被观测'; 实验证据目前只有低阶谐波的非经典关联(PRX Quantum 5, 040319).

### 研究方法（Methods）

- Moment-matrix PPT construction on the partially transposed IR mode using f1 = a2 a0, f2 = a1 a0+ - turning RABBIT fringe amplitude into a quadratic entanglement witness with the exact operator ordering fixed by the proof
- Fock-sector decomposition of the three-mode coherence (qL for t = 2, r = 0; RA, RB for the rest) with sector-wise Cauchy-Schwarz bounds over all three bipartition cuts and their convex mixtures - the technique behind the GME bound H(U,V)
- Spectral-shaping tightening: using gratings/monochromator masks to remove adjacent harmonics so sidebands SB+/- read U, V directly instead of upper bounds U_dc, V_dc - tightening the certification threshold from |Q|^2 > U_dc V_dc to |Q|^2 > UV
- Three-sideband certification protocol: central sideband for Q, outer sidebands for U, V - entanglement certification from a single RABBIT spectrogram with no auxiliary optical measurements

### 主要发现与核心结论（Main Results）

- Quantum-optical RABBIT mapping: the central sideband SB0 gives SB0(tau) = g1^2 D1 + g2^2 D2 + g1g2*2 Re(<Q e^{2i w0 tau}>), with D1 = <N1 N0>, D2 = <N2(N0+1)> and the three-mode coherence Q = <a2+ a1 a0^2>; the oscillating RABBIT fringe directly encodes Q - photoelectron interferometry measures optical correlation functions, not just classical delays
- No-go theorem: from Cauchy-Schwarz |Q|^2 <= D1 D2 holds for ALL states (entangled or separable), and stronger, every tuple (Q, D1, D2) admits a fully separable explanation (squeezed-vacuum IR with <N0> = 1 + coherent upper harmonic + phase-mixed lower harmonic) - so a single sideband fringe can never certify entanglement, no matter the violation pattern
- IR-XUV entanglement witness: the outer sidebands supply U = <N2 N0> and V = <N1(N0+1)>; the moment matrix of the partially transposed state (f1 = a2 a0, f2 = a1 a0+) gives det G = UV - |Q|^2 >= 0 for every PPT state, so |Q|^2 > UV certifies NPT entanglement across IR | XUV - the operator ordering (N0+1 in V) is dictated by the partial transpose and cannot be replaced by D1, D2
- Genuine tripartite (GME) witness: for every biseparable three-mode state |Q| <= H(U,V) = sqrt((2-sqrt(3))V) + sqrt((1+sqrt(2))U(V+1)) + sqrt(2 sqrt(2) U V); violation certifies GME among (IR, XUV1, XUV2). Proof decomposes Q = qL + RA + RB by Fock sectors (a0+ a1 a2+ annihilates two IR photons, so coherences start at t >= 2) and bounds each sector by positivity + Cauchy-Schwarz, separately for the three cuts and their mixtures
- Optimality at U = 0: when spectral shaping of the XUV removes the a+/- harmonics so the outer sidebands read U and V directly (SB+ = g2^2 U, SB- = g1^2 V), every biseparable state obeys |Q|^2 <= (2-sqrt(3))V - and an explicit biseparable construction (|alpha>_1 (x) [z* |2,0> + e^{i phi} sqrt(1-z*^2)|0,1>]_02 with opposite-phase mixing) fills the entire disk, making the criterion necessary AND sufficient for the measured moments (Q, V)
- Benchmark on explicit states: displaced two-mode squeezed vacuum D0(alpha) sum z^k|k,k>_01 (x) |beta>_2 - the state predicted in HHG - is certified by the RABBIT witness for 0 < n < (4 sqrt(2)-2)/7 and x-(n) < x < x+(n), strictly inside the full Gaussian-PPT entangled region (nu- < 1/2 for all n > 0), quantifying witness sensitivity; non-Gaussian family S0(r)[sqrt(p)|0,0,1> + sqrt(1-p)|2,1,0>] is GME for all 0 < p < 1 but certified only for 0 <= r < r_c = 0.04294 at p = 0.75 (local IR squeezing dilutes the witness)
- Extension to XUV-XUV entanglement: with an independently prepared coherent IR reference, Q factorizes as Q = C0 C21 with C21 = <a2+ a1>, and the Hillery-Zubahir-type criterion |C21|^2 > J21 = <N2 N1> certifies entanglement between two harmonics - the cross-population J21 requires electron coincidence detection or auxiliary optical measurement beyond standard RABBIT

### 潜在研究方向（Future Directions）

- Immediate experimental target: apply the three-sideband protocol to standard RABBIT setups on HHG sources - the required observables (sideband constants + fringe amplitude) are already recorded in conventional spectrograms, so existing data may be reanalyzed
- XUV-XUV certification needs electron coincidence detection (J21 = <N2 N1>) or auxiliary optics - a clear instrumentation development direction for attosecond quantum optics
- The witness detects only a subregion of entangled states (e.g. r_c = 0.04294 for the GME family) - systematic exploration of the detectable state families and bound tightening is open
- Connecting certification to spectroscopy: entanglement structure between IR and harmonics affects matter spectroscopy signals (frequency correlations as a control knob), so certified entanglement becomes a quantitative resource estimate for quantum-light-driven spectroscopy
- Program context: this closes the measurement gap in the strong-field quantum optics program (Stammer-Lewenstein line) - generation (PRL 132, 143603) is predicted, now certification is specified; the remaining step is an actual experiment
