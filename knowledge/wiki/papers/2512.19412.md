## 深度解析

### 研究问题（Research Question）

First demonstration of few-cycle mid-infrared (3.2 um) perfect optical vortex (POV) beams and their use to drive HHG in ZnO and GaSe. POV beams keep their annular intensity distribution invariant with topological charge, solving the beam-growth problem that has capped OAM values in nonlinear vortex experiments. Harmonic vortices with topological charge up to 36 are produced by OAM up-scaling in the non-perturbative process - the largest TC harmonic vortices reported, in the IR-to-visible range.

### 研究背景（Background）

Directly relevant to project A's material platform (ZnO a-cut, the same crystal cut and thickness class used in ZnO HHG literature) and to the structured-EUV direction of the order-parameter program. The TC-doubling in SHG and TC-conservation up-scaling in HHG give a clean symmetry benchmark: OAM adds as a spectator quantum number through the perturbative-to-nonperturbative crossover in these crystals. For project A specifically: a vortex driver modifies the intraband current geometry (transverse phase gradient adds an effective angular component to the electron excursion) - whether sigma_solid's Berry-connection term survives structured-drive averaging is an open, tractable question. The paper also complements 2609.38727: metasurface resonant enhancement (spatial structuring of the medium) versus free-space vortex structuring of the driver - two orthogonal knobs on the same solid-HHG process.

### 研究方法（Methods）

Passive MIR vortex-beam generation chain (SPP + axicon + lens); POV asymptotic-regime design rule (r0/delta > 10 keeps TC-independent intensity); cascaded SPPs for high-TC fundamentals; TC measurement of harmonic vortices (supplementary double-slit / interferometric methods)

### 主要发现与核心结论（Main Results）

- Passive MIR beam shaping: spiral phase plate + axicon + lens generates fs POV and Bessel-Gauss vortex beams at 3.2 um with few-cycle pulse structure preserved - no SLM needed (efficiency/wavelength/bandwidth limited), all-passive elements robust enough for strong-field intensities
- POV TC-invariance verified experimentally: ring radius 2r0 ~ 415 um constant within error for TC -3 to +3 (ratio 2r0/2delta ~ 48.8), while BGV beam radius grows linearly with TC as expected
- SHG in ZnO (a-cut 270 um) and GaSe (100 um): harmonic TC exactly doubles the driver TC (l2 = 2*l0 verified for l0 = 1, -3, -5 in ZnO and 3, 5, 6 in GaSe) - OAM conservation holds for POV-driven perturbative nonlinearity
- HHG (5th-7th harmonics): GaSe reaches harmonic TC lq = 35 (q=6) and 36 (q=7) driven by fundamental l0 = 5 and 6 via cascaded SPPs - linear lq vs l0 trend confirms OAM conservation in the non-perturbative regime
- Solid-state HHG with structured light works at ambient conditions - a practical advantage over gas-phase vortex HHG which requires vacuum

### 潜在研究方向（Future Directions）

Higher harmonic orders with POV drivers (current demonstration stops at 7th); helical dichroism applications suggested by authors (molecular switches/motors in liquids sensitive to OAM sign); coupling OAM-structured drivers to anisotropic HHG selection rules in ZnO (crystal-orientation-dependent OAM transfer)
